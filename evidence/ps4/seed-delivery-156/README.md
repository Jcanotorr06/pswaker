# Audited expanded seed delivery (#156)

This record consolidates the six bounded #150–#155 expansion inventories, their
retained collections and final native reruns. It delivers the exact union through
the private required assembly-assets workflow and a rebuilt Windows desktop
bundle. **Complete content coverage and physical acceptance remain unresolved.**
No owner acceptance of the important generation gaps is inferred. This slice
does not modify #136 or publish a release.

The frozen union has **493 descriptions**: all 301 baseline records plus 192
distinct additions. Final consolidation reproduced the complete database,
provenance manifest, validation reports and union audit byte-for-byte. All 49
exercised scenarios reproduced their earlier draw/output fields; no descriptions
were lost or conflicted. The 214 fresh inventory rows comprise 49 generated
exercises, two justified shared mappings, 112 unsupported rows and 51 missing
rows. The full image has 1,321 archives: 25 have partial inventory references and
1,296 have no resource/state recipe. Unexercised contents of referenced archives
remain gaps too. These are substantial unresolved coverage limits.
Independent extraction of the canonical desktop package passed 1,090 identity
and full-description comparisons from all 98 retained/final observation assets.

validation. Missing/conflicting descriptions or observed missing-pipeline waits
