## Reproduce privately

With the retained private artifact and platform roots selected, replay each
original database/WAL pair with the existing snapshot command into a **new**
directory, then compare the standalone validated assets:

```bash
python3 tools/pswaker/complete-package.py pipeline-seed /private/cold/pipeline_cache.db \
  --output "$PRIVATE_ROOT/new-cold-snapshot"
python3 tools/pswaker/complete-package.py pipeline-seed /private/warm/pipeline_cache.db \
  --output "$PRIVATE_ROOT/new-warm-snapshot"
python3 evidence/ps4/outset-coverage-111/verify-coverage.py \
  "$PRIVATE_ROOT/performance-22-seed-01.13" \
  "$PRIVATE_ROOT/new-cold-snapshot" "$PRIVATE_ROOT/new-warm-snapshot" \
  --output "$PRIVATE_ROOT/new-coverage.json"
python3 tools/pswaker/complete-package.py inspect \
  "$PRIVATE_ROOT/outset-coverage-111-complete-01.20" --platform-root "$PLATFORM_ROOT"
```

Snapshot order is cold then warm in the committed report. Compare the generated
JSON to [coverage.json](coverage.json); it contains no input-directory paths.
Keep the source pairs stable and retain all earlier artifacts unchanged.

## Console handoff and limits

Use the identified **#111 package**, with its matching ELF and fresh inspection,
for the final #107 comparison. It includes the #110 preparation implementation;
it has not been played on the PS4. Follow
[shader preparation](../../../docs/ps4/SHADER-PREPARATION.md#console-handoff)
and the [action checklist](../../../docs/ps4/Action-baseline-checklist.md):

- Keep the same Outset save and applied controls; assume Hero's Sword and shield,
  no items. Start outdoors on open dry ground near the shore. Record an exact
  landmark and walk before testing: the baseline return did not identify them.
- The baseline's planned two-minute timeline is 0–10 s still, 10–40 s five
  rolls about 5 s apart, 40–70 s five standing sword swings about 5 s apart,
  70–110 s water entry and about 30 s near-shore swimming with turns, then
  110–125 s shore/shallows and stillness. Actual first occurrences must be timed
  and filmed in the new test, including the approach; no gameplay warmup.
- Record PS4 model/firmware/GoldHEN, installed package/build/seed, empty disposable
  cache, startup/preparation, first actions, repetitions and retained-cache
  relaunch separately. Preserve saves/settings and existing caches. Normal Quit
  must precede database/WAL retrieval. Record the complete preparation cost,
  including overlap with earlier startup, and the final drain separately.
- Compare shader waits and per-action frame-time spikes against the accepted
  unseeded baseline. Its missing action timings, undescribed walk, one sample
  per launch, absent backup/cache-rename and normal-Quit evidence, probable live
  warm database copy and unverified physical timing remain limitations. New
  notes cannot retroactively supply those baseline observations.
- Check effect appearance, quality, controls and audio. Preserve residual stalls
  without shader waits for separate investigation. Successful inspection,
  preparation completion and counts do not prove smooth gameplay or physical
  presentation timing. This covers only recorded configurations on this Outset
  route; other islands, forest, smoke/explosions and full-game coverage are not
  established. New equipment, camera or action combinations can need more work.
